📖 Benchmark Explainers

GPQA Diamond

448 graduate-level multiple-choice questions in biology, chemistry, and physics. Designed to be impossible to answer by web search alone. PhD specialists score ~65%, making this a true test of deep reasoning. Now the primary discriminator between frontier models.

Claude Opus 4.7 — 94.2%  |  Gemini 3.1 Pro — 94.3%

MATH-500

A curated 500-problem subset of the MATH dataset featuring competition-level mathematics from AMC, AIME and above. Tests multi-step symbolic reasoning, not pattern matching. Reasoning models using chain-of-thought score significantly higher.

DeepSeek R1 — 97.3%  |  o3-pro — 96.7%

SWE-bench Verified

500 real GitHub issues from popular Python repositories that the model must resolve end-to-end by writing patches that pass the existing test suite. The closest benchmark to production software engineering. Highly resistant to training-data contamination.

Claude Opus 4.7 — 87.6%  |  Gemini 3.1 Pro — 80.6%

HumanEval

164 hand-written Python programming problems evaluated with unit tests. Uses Pass@1 metric. Nearly saturated at frontier (90-95%), but remains a useful baseline for small and mid-sized models. Problems are interview-level difficulty.

GPT-5.4 — 94.1%  |  Kimi K2.6 — ~94.5%

MMLU

57-subject multiple-choice benchmark covering STEM, humanities, law, medicine and more. Largely saturated above 88% for frontier models and no longer meaningfully differentiates the top tier. Still useful as a knowledge baseline for smaller models.

Claude Opus 4.6 — 92.1%  |  GPT-5.5 — ~92%

MT-Bench

80 multi-turn conversation questions evaluated by GPT-4 on a 1–10 scale. Tests instruction following, coherence and reasoning across topics like writing, coding, math and roleplay. Scores above 9.0 now indicate top-tier chat capability.

GPT-5.4 — 9.5  |  GPT-5.5 — 9.5

AIME 2025

All 30 problems from the 2025 American Invitational Mathematics Examination, released after most training cutoffs. Olympiad-level short-answer problems. A single incorrect answer costs ~3.3%, making it a high-resolution math-reasoning signal.

Grok 4 — 100%  |  GPT-5.2 & o3-pro — 100%

GSM8K

8,500 grade-school math word problems requiring multi-step arithmetic. Largely saturated for frontier models at 95%+. Now mainly used as a sanity-check for baseline math reasoning in smaller and fine-tuned models.

o3-pro — 99.1%  |  GPT-5.4 — 98.1%

🎯 Model Selection Guide

🧬 Scientific Research & Deep Reasoning

Gemini 3.1 Pro or Claude Opus 4.7 — both score 94%+ on GPQA Diamond. Gemini leads on multimodal science (charts, PDFs, figures); Claude leads on long-form synthesis with 1M context.

💻 Software Engineering Agents

Claude Opus 4.7 leads SWE-bench at 87.6%. Kimi K2.6 and GLM-5.1 are the best open-weight alternatives. GPT-5.4 and Grok 4 are close seconds for closed APIs.

🔢 Mathematics & Olympiad Problems

o3-pro leads MATH-500 at 96.7%. DeepSeek R1 tops at 97.3% for open-weight. Grok 4 achieves 100% on AIME 2025. All three use chain-of-thought reasoning.

💲 Best Value / Cost Efficiency

DeepSeek V4 at $0.28/$0.42 per 1M tokens delivers near-frontier quality. Kimi K2.6 is cheapest in the top-10 GPQA ranking at $0.95/M. Self-hosting Gemma 4 26B has zero token costs.

🖥️ Local / On-Device Deployment

Gemma 4 26B runs at 85 t/s on consumer hardware (14 GB RAM). Phi-4 (14B, MIT) beats models 3× its size on math. Qwen3 32B is the best single-GPU balance of quality and speed.

📄 Long Document / RAG Workloads

Llama 4 Scout offers a 10M-token context window — unmatched for RAG over entire codebases or document archives. Grok 4 provides 2M tokens for closed-API long-doc tasks.

🌐 Multilingual Applications

Qwen3.5 supports 201 languages with Apache 2.0 licensing. Mistral Large 3 covers 80+ languages and is Europe's strongest open-weight choice for regulated environments.

🏢 Enterprise / Compliance

For strict data residency: self-host DeepSeek R1 (MIT), Qwen3 (Apache 2.0) or Mistral Large 3 (Apache 2.0). All allow commercial fine-tuning with no royalties or geographic restrictions.
